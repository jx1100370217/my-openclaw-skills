# Market Research Tools for iOS Apps

## App Store Intelligence

### Sensor Tower
- **URL:** https://sensortower.com
- **Features:** Downloads, revenue, keywords, ASO
- **Pricing:** Free tier limited, paid for full data

### data.ai (App Annie)
- **URL:** https://data.ai
- **Features:** Market data, competitor analysis
- **Pricing:** Enterprise-focused

### AppMagic
- **URL:** https://appmagic.rocks
- **Features:** Downloads, revenue estimates
- **Pricing:** Subscription-based

## Keyword Research

### App Store Connect
- **Free:** See your own search terms
- **Use:** Understand how users find you

### AppTweak
- **URL:** https://www.apptweak.com
- **Features:** Keyword volume, difficulty
- **Pricing:** Subscription

### Mobile Action
- **URL:** https://www.mobileaction.co
- **Features:** ASO tools, keyword tracking

## User Research

### UserTesting
- **URL:** https://www.usertesting.com
- **Use:** Get video feedback from real users

### Maze
- **URL:** https://maze.co
- **Use:** Prototype testing

### Typeform
- **URL:** https://www.typeform.com
- **Use:** Surveys, user interviews

## Market Data

### Statista
- **URL:** https://www.statista.com
- **Use:** Industry statistics, market size

### Google Trends
- **URL:** https://trends.google.com
- **Use:** Search interest over time

### Crunchbase
- **URL:** https://www.crunchbase.com
- **Use:** Competitor funding, company data

## Free Alternatives

### App Store Reviews
- Manual analysis of competitor reviews
- Use for pain points, feature requests

### Reddit
- r/iOSProgramming, r/Apple, niche subreddits
- Great for user sentiment

### Twitter/X
- Search "[app name]" for real feedback

### Product Hunt
- See new app launches, upvotes, comments

## Research Workflow

1. **Identify Keywords** → Google Trends, App Store search
2. **Size the Market** → Statista, industry reports
3. **Analyze Competitors** → Sensor Tower, App Store
4. **Validate with Users** → Reddit, surveys, interviews
5. **Document Findings** → Create validation report
